# Banks never ask for KYC through links or OTPs on a call

Your bank will not call or message you to update KYC through a link, and will
never ask for an OTP, PIN, CVV, card number or account password. Messages
warning that your account "will be blocked today" unless you click a link are
phishing. When in doubt, contact your bank using the number printed on your
card or passbook.

Source: RBI / bank KYC-fraud advisory
