# There is no such thing as a digital arrest

Police, the CBI, the ED, customs officials and TRAI never arrest anyone over a
phone call or a video call. They do not keep you on a call for hours, do not
place you under "digital arrest" or "digital surveillance", and never ask you
to transfer money to a "safe account" to prove your innocence. Any caller who
claims otherwise is a fraudster reading from a script.

If you receive such a call: stay calm, do not share personal or bank details,
disconnect, and report it on 1930.

Source: I4C / Ministry of Home Affairs advisory on digital arrest scams
