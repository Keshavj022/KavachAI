# Agencies never settle a case by taking your money

No genuine investigation is resolved by moving your savings to an account a
caller provides. Demands for RTGS, NEFT or UPI transfers, "security deposits",
"verification of funds" or "bail" paid over a call are hallmarks of fraud. A
real notice arrives in writing through official channels, and you are never
asked to keep it secret from your family.

Source: RBI customer-awareness guidance
